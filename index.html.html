Progression

  • Initially leave 2–3 reps in reserve on most sets.
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets with good form, increase load slightly.
  • For bodyweight moves, progress reps, control or difficulty.
  • Rest 2–3 minutes on compound lifts, 60–120 seconds on accessories.
  • Keep steps consistent and adjust nutrition after 2–3 weeks of trend data.
